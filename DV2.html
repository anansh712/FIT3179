Australian Health Insights Dashboard (FIT3179)

Visualising national patterns in obesity, activity, BMI, and nutrition across Australia (ABS & AIHW, 2022).

1. Obesity Rates by State

Choropleth map showing the percentage of adults classified as obese in each state. Darker shades indicate higher prevalence; Tasmania and Queensland tend higher than Western Australia.

2. Physical Activity by Age

Grouped bars show the share of people reporting different days per week of physical activity. Activity levels are generally higher in younger groups and decrease with age.

3. BMI Classification by Age & Sex

Compare proportions in Underweight, Normal, Overweight, and Obese across age groups. Use the dropdown inside the chart to toggle Male/Female.

4. Fruit & Vegetable Serves

Dumbbell plot compares average daily fruit vs vegetable serves by age. Most groups fall short of guideline targets (~2 fruit & ~5 veg/day).
